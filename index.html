About Me

I am a Mixtec researcher, linguist, educator, and interpreter specializing in Mixtec languages. My work lies at the intersection of language documentation, Indigenous education, community-based research methodologies, and language technology.

Since 2016, I have worked on the documentation of the San Martín Peras Mixtec language and have also contributed to the documentation of other Mixtec variants. My work includes contributing to the development of the San Martín Peras Mixtec orthography, transcribing hundreds of hours of Mixtec narratives, oral histories, conversations, and traditional stories, and translating educational materials and books into the San Martín Peras variety of Mixtec. I also work as a trilingual interpreter in Mixtec, Spanish, and English and have served as a consultant on projects related to Indigenous languages, culture, language documentation, and community engagement.

I lead language documentation and revitalization initiatives with Mixtec-speaking communities in California. My work emphasizes community-based and collaborative approaches to language revitalization, the development of educational resources for Mixtec-speaking communities, and advocacy for linguistic diversity and the rights of Indigenous peoples.

My primary research focuses on syntax, phonology, semantic typology, Indigenous language documentation, and the development of technologies that support Indigenous languages. I am particularly interested in how linguistic research and digital tools can strengthen language revitalization efforts while remaining accountable to the communities they serve.

As a Mixtec educator, I am passionate about teaching, translation, interpreting, and facilitating workshops on Indigenous languages. I am committed to creating accessible resources that bridge academic research and community knowledge, ensuring that scholarship is both meaningful and beneficial to the communities it represents.

Outside of my academic and community work, I enjoy trail running, gravel cycling, hiking, and Muay Thai. I am also passionate about mountains, and one of my long-term goals is to climb Aconcagua, the highest mountain in the Americas.
